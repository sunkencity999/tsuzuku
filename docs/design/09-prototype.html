つづく tsuzukuto be continued · the book that takes ink

Everything is printed. Paper has grain; ink bleeds, then dries overnight; a seal is pressed and leaves a slightly imperfect mark; the page carries the real date and the colour of the paper follows the calendar. Edo is the hand, the arcade is a woodblock print of an arcade, the status window is the print's cartouche. Three eras, one press, differing in tempo, not colour. Toggle the theme, reduced motion and slow motion above; every demo replays.

§4.3 · The calendar mechanism · deterministic from the date

暦koyomi · the calendarSky, sekki, moon

The real hour paints the sky; the date names one of twenty-four sekki (traditional calendar, approximate) and tints the paper by at most 3 %. Shown as dates, never countdowns. Nothing unlocks on a season; it is decorative with a label.

Moment H · 節気 sekki · the calendar turns · the paper tint steps (≤ 3 %), the label kasure/nijimi, the moon changes

Contrast readouts measure --fg-muted on the tinted paper with the WCAG formula from tokens.test.ts. Every pair must stay ≥ 4.5:1. The whole page body is tinted when you press a date (one full-page paint, accepted once a fortnight).

§6.1 · Motion tokens · durations and eases

動dō · motionTokens

Edo moves at 400–2,400 ms, the arcade at 40–220 ms, the cartouche at 220–600 ms. Reduced motion zeroes --dur-1…5 and --stagger and keeps only --dur-fade (150 ms). Slow motion multiplies every token by 4.

Durations · one dot, --ease

Eases · one dot, --dur-4

Composited properties only: opacity, transform, clip-path, stroke-dashoffset. Never width, height, box-shadow, text-shadow or filter.

§6.2–6.3 · Seven ink verbs · the choreography grammar

七nana · sevenGrammar board

Every animation in the app is one of seven verbs, so a review can say "that should be a nijimi, not an osu". Each has a reduced-motion form: keep opacity, drop travel, drop loops, nothing longer than 150 ms.

§7 E · Ōin · a stamp or badge · and the Hanko Button

押osu · to pressSeal press

The sheet bleeds in with its grain; the seal presses on the spring; the wet ink dries over the dry layer (kawaku); the era date appears in Mincho. Today's ink stays wet (30 %) until the 04:00 roll; tomorrow it is dry. That is the "what's new" layer: no counts, no notification.

§7 I · Bokuten · the ink dot · §7 J · Michi · step to step · §7 F · Shiori · about to leave

点ten · a dotThe rep loop

Correct: a matcha ink dot blooms at the tile's mark. Wrong: a dry-brush strike draws across the text (ink, not red). The ki meter draws from the left and a full bar charges the hanko. Steps change with a 6 px dry-out left and an 8 px bleed-in right, tiles staggered 40 ms. Under Arcade feel a single 33 ms hit-stop. The loop never waits on decoration.

Colophon · what this page is and is not

奥oku · the back of the bookNotes

  • One static HTML page, no build, no dependencies. Fonts come from Google Fonts here; the app self-hosts subsets (and the twelve sekki glyphs need the one rebuild §3.5 describes).
  • Sound is never played on this page. The app's recipes (tsuzuku, stamp, promotion…) are named where they would fire.
  • The 72 kō are shown for the four sekki used in the demos; the rest are data for koyomi.json and await a Sensei-voice review.
  • Glows are counted live in Arcade Night from the [data-glow] twins in the viewport; the ceiling is 6.
  • Nothing here flashes above 3 Hz; the only loops are the flame flicker (1.6–1.7 s) and the lantern breath (4 s), both off under reduced motion.
