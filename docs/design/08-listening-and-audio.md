## 1. Thesis

A learner who starts with zero Japanese should be able to reach fluency with the dojo as the main road and the world as the supplement, not the other way round. Before this design every voice in the app was the device's `speechSynthesis`, three stations in four held one dialogue, nothing played with the screen off, and the Perapera 耳 axis was held at 70 until the learner logged 20 hours of listening *outside*. This design turns listening into three things the app already knows how to make — **content** (100 audio-first pieces through the same validator and linker as every text), **mined input** (the 12,775 example sentences — 8,710 of them in the pools — 1,892 grammar examples and 3,268 story lines graded at the learner's live known set) and **a voice** (a pre-rendered VOICEVOX pack keyed by content id, with per-mora timing) — and re-points the honesty copy at what is now true: the 耳 cap steps 70 → 85 → 100 at 7.5 h and 15 h of authentic-style listening heard in the dojo *or* outside (§12).

Three promises hold throughout: the voices are **synthetic and say so** everywhere the learner meets them (every status chip, header and credit line says *synthetic*; the `(synthetic)` flag stays unconditional); the app **never blocks on the pack** (every surface falls back to `speechSynthesis` per clip, and the site builds with no pack); and the in-app share of the 1,500-hour goal is **stated, not implied** — the shipped copy says about 31 h of sentences and texts (plus about 3 h of word and phrase clips that serve drills, not the ledger) and about 60–75 h of worthwhile hearing over the road (§12; measured from the published core + pool pack `a-3467889b1f` on 2026-10-18 — 30.93 h of connected speech, 2.80 h of drill clips; the ear tier is not rendered yet).

## 4. Mining: how sentences become graded listening

1. **Segment and link at build time.** `scripts/linker.ts` gains `segmentSentence(pool, plain)` — a greedy longest-match over `pool.exact ∪ pool.forms ∪ FUNCTION_TOKENS ∪ CAST_NAMES` with `matchToken` for stems, hints and counters; unknown runs stay one token — and `linkSentence(pool, ex)` → `{ ids: string[]; unknown: number }`. Calibrated against the stories' own `tokenIds` (3,268 lines; `docs/listening-report.md` §4): the all-known verdict agrees on 94.3 % of lines, the linked id set is identical on 58.0 %, the vocab ids alone on 63.6 % — the differences are the writers' tokenisation of grammar surfaces (とても, ように, のため kept whole or split) and the V20 hint rule on unknown verbs; story lines enter the pools by their writers' `tokenIds`, not by this segmenter. `linkSentence(...).unknown === 0` is the arbiter of V46 (CONTENT_GUIDE §18): an item that passes V46 always has an own-station pool sentence.
2. **Emit pools, not tokens.** Tokens are **not** written into `SentenceExample` (they would add ≈ 3.6 MB and push 16 item files over `MAX_FILE_BYTES`). `content:assign` writes `public/content/listening/pool-NN.json` (`ListeningPool`, schema `tsuzuku.pool/1`, new `CONTENT_KINDS` entry `'pool'`, listed in `manifest.json` like `stations/NN.json`): one `PoolRef = { ref, ids, kana, morae }` per sentence whose every content token links inside the cumulative allowlist and whose `knownAt` equals NN — **`knownAt = max(owner station, stations of its ids)`**: an example sits no earlier than its item's station even when its words are all older (the item is still the sentence's point), a story line no earlier than its text's station (`max(text station, stations of its ids)`), and an example the segmenter only covers with a later pool's surfaces at that pool's station. `ref` is the clip key (§8): `v-n5-0249#ex0`, `g-n5-024#ex1`, `s-07-2#L3`; `morae` is `speechMorae(kana).length` (`src/shared/jp.ts`, the body the validator's V41 count and the clip indexes' `m` / `c` cues share) and `kana` stays in the ref because Radio reads it (lead decision). A sentence with an out-of-corpus token enters no pool (it is a rewrite candidate: 4,065 examples, listed in `docs/listening-report.md` §5). Landed: 53 pools, **16,188 refs** (5,947 story lines · 1,531 grammar examples · 8,710 vocab examples), 1,125 min of own-station speech, 4,280 KiB on disk; the largest file is Station 43 (583 refs, 178 KiB) and 28 files are over 80 KB. **`POOL_FILE_BUDGET_BYTES` = 256 KiB is a hard limit**: `content:index`'s default check throws a `BuildIndexError` naming the file, its bytes and the budget (`oversizePools`; `BuildIndexOptions.poolFileBudget` for tests), so an oversize pool fails CI exactly like a V44 finding. Pool files are generated, never hand-written, and are **not ContentFiles**: `scripts/validate-content.ts` skips `listening/pool-*.json` in its walk exactly as it skips `stations/` (otherwise V08 fails on the unknown directory), and CONTENT_GUIDE §1 lists the row.
3. **Select at the learner's live known set.** `knownSet` (`src/learn/known.ts`, base-facet R ≥ 0.85 — the same set `gatekeeper.stationSentences` and `params.knownSentences` use) over the pools ≤ station: **i+0** = every id known; **i+1** = exactly one id unknown **and that id's `listen` card exists** and is due within `IMPLICIT_WINDOW_DAYS` (3) or not yet in review state — so the one unknown is the word FSRS wants rehearsed, and `implicitListenGrades` (`src/input/listening/set.ts:255`, which writes nothing for a missing card) records the evidence. An unknown id whose `listen` card has not been created yet (vocab `listen` unlocks at rec S ≥ 2 d, `src/learn/scheduler.ts`) is *not* i+1. Pool sizes are ceilings: in a station's first weeks the R filter admits a fraction of them, so the Station page shows the live count ("28 min you can follow"), never the allowlist figure.
4. **Consumers.** The Ear Walk (§10.4), Radio (§10.2), `sentence-listen` planning (an i+0 example of the item first, then a story line, then the bare word), Gatekeeper 耳 and Shinsa 耳 (own-station pool refs before the bare-word top-up), Shadow warm-up (`knownSentences` reads pool refs instead of `examples[0]`).

## 5. Schema and validator

Additions to `src/content/types.ts` (contract tier; CHANGELOG line):

```ts
export const LISTENING_GENRES = ['exchange','self-intro','phone','directions','voicemail','casual-talk','narrative','announcement','weather','interview','news','drama','debate','lecture','essay'] as const;
export type ListeningGenre = (typeof LISTENING_GENRES)[number];
export const GENRE_FROM_STATION: Record<ListeningGenre, number> = { exchange: 4, 'self-intro': 4, phone: 8, directions: 10, voicemail: 10, 'casual-talk': 10, narrative: 16, announcement: 16, weather: 16, interview: 25, news: 32, drama: 32, debate: 39, lecture: 39, essay: 47 };
export const AUTHENTIC_GENRES: ReadonlySet<ListeningGenre> = new Set(['casual-talk','interview','news','drama','debate','lecture']);
/** Authentic-style text (design 08 §12): a bridge text, an AUTHENTIC_GENRES piece, or a casual dialogue (the 29 existing ones from Station 10). */
export const isAuthenticText = (s: Pick<StoryItem, 'bridge' | 'genre' | 'kind' | 'register'>): boolean =>
  !!s.bridge || (!!s.genre && AUTHENTIC_GENRES.has(s.genre)) || (s.kind === 'dialogue' && s.register === 'casual');
export type LineEmotion = 'neutral'|'warm'|'annoyed'|'sad'|'glad'|'whisper';
export type QuestionKind = 'gist'|'detail'|'number'|'speaker'|'inference';
// StoryItem: genre?: ListeningGenre; audioFirst?: true; narrator?: string (cast name of a speaker-less text's reader)
// StoryLine: emotion?: LineEmotion
// ComprehensionQuestion: kind?: QuestionKind; lineRef?: number (index into lines; a miss replays this line)
// StationQuota: listening: number (0 for Stations 1–3, 2 for 4–53)
// CONTENT_KINDS += 'pool'; ID_PATTERNS.pool = /^pool-(0[1-9]|[1-4]\d|5[0-3])$/
export interface PoolRef { ref: string; ids: string[]; kana: string; morae: number }   // morae = speechMorae(kana).length; a pool file is ≤ POOL_FILE_BUDGET_BYTES (256 KiB, hard; §4)
export interface ListeningPool { schema: 'tsuzuku.pool/1'; station: number; refs: PoolRef[] }
```

`StoryKind` stays `'story' | 'dialogue'`: a news bulletin, lecture, one-sided phone call or voicemail is a speaker-less `story` with `narrator`; a two-sided phone call or debate is a `dialogue`. `isStoryItem` is shape-based, so the Arena, ledger, XP and badges consume pieces unchanged; `kindOfId`, the loader, `StationBundle` and the manifest need nothing new for pieces. Role-play and Shinsa 口 select `kind === 'dialogue' && (!audioFirst || learnerSpeaker)` (§11): a piece enters speaking surfaces only when its writer sets `learnerSpeaker`, and then V27's learner-line fields apply to it.

Validator rules (CONTENT_GUIDE §18; F = fail, W = warn):

- **V41** F · Listening fields: `genre ∈ LISTENING_GENRES` and `GENRE_FROM_STATION[genre] ≤ station`; `audioFirst` only with `genre`; `narrator` only on `kind: 'story'`; `emotion ∈ LINE_EMOTIONS` (W on a `story` line without `narrator`); `comprehension[].kind ∈ QUESTION_KINDS`, `lineRef` a valid line index, `kind: 'speaker'` only in a dialogue; an `audioFirst` text has ≥ 1 `gist` question and, through Station 15, every line ≤ 22 morae (`PIECE_LINE_MAX_MORAE`, counted with `speechMorae` from `src/shared/jp.ts` — punctuation is never a mora); an `audioFirst` dialogue with `learnerSpeaker` names one of its own speakers (then V27 applies), without it V27 is not required.

## 7. The audio pack pipeline

`render → verify → encode → sprites → indexes → manifest → release asset → CI fetch`; every step is incremental, content-hash keyed and resumable. Scripts live in `scripts/audio/` and run on the build Mac (engine at `http://127.0.0.1:50021…50024`, four processes ≈ 2.1× realtime); CI never renders.

1. **Inventory** (`render-audio.ts --tier core|pool|ear --stations 1-17 --engines … --dry-run --fixtures`). Text handed to the engine is `textToSpeak(toPlain(ja), kana)` so the clip and the caller agree. `textToSpeak` and `stripDisplayMarks` move from `src/audio/tts.ts` to a pure contract-tier module **`src/shared/speak-text.ts`** (imports with `.ts` extensions, no store or signals import; `tts.ts` and `@/audio` re-export them), because Node cannot import `tts.ts` today — it imports `'../content/ruby'` without the extension and `{ store } from '../store'`. ARCHITECTURE §0 adds `src/shared/speak-text.ts` and `src/shared/jp.ts` to the modules scripts may import, and `scripts/audio/render-audio.test.ts` asserts `import()` of every consumed module succeeds under Node. ## 8. Runtime

**ClipPlayer** (`src/audio/clips.ts`, zero dependencies):

**Playback and iOS.** WebKit's autoplay unlock is per media element, so `unlock()` — called synchronously inside the existing first-gesture `warmUp()` (`src/audio/tts.ts:508`) — constructs the **fixed pool of 6 `Audio` elements once**, sets each `src` to a tiny silent Opus blob and calls `play().then(pause)` inside the gesture; from then on the LRU only swaps `src` (a blob URL from `fetch('./audio/sprites/…')`, re-wrapped as `new Blob([buf], { type: 'audio/webm; codecs=opus' })` because Pages serves `.webm` as `video/webm`) and never calls `new Audio()` again. `play()` before `unlock()` resolves `done` at once with `source: 'none'` so callers fall back to the device voice instead of hanging. `preservesPitch = true` (`webkitPreservesPitch` on old Safari); `playbackRate = rate / cue.rate`; `currentTime = start`. **Played seconds come from media events only**: `currentTime` deltas accumulated on `timeupdate` (fires with the screen off on both engines), reconciled at `pause` / `ended` / `visibilitychange`; the stop at `cue.end` is driven by `timeupdate` with a `(end − start) / rate + 1 s` timer only as a foreground backstop; `requestAnimationFrame` drives the karaoke sweep and nothing else. A whole-text Radio track never schedules a stop timer — the sprite plays edge to edge and `ended` advances the playlist. A text with a skipped line has no whole track (`whole: null` in `sNN/stories.json`; the runtime never synthesises one from the line cues' min / max), so Radio skips it while its present lines still play by key and the device voice covers the skipped one. `pause()` / `resume()` hold the element in place (the stop backstop and the karaoke sweep pause with it; a pause before the sprite arrived keeps the element silent until resume); `done.seconds` counts both halves once in media seconds and `position()` reads the seconds played so far without firing `onPlayed`. Blob URLs bypass the service worker, so Safari's Range/206 requirement for media from the Cache API never arises.

**Clip keys** (`SpeakOptions.clip`): `<itemId>` for a word or kana, `<itemId>#ex<k>` for an example, `<storyId>#L<i>` for a line, `<itemId>#def`, `<itemId>#col<k>`, `<itemId>#ga`. Every caller that holds the item passes the key (`speakWord` → `item.id`; StoryReader, Arena, RolePlay → `${story.id}#L${i}`; the planner sets `StepParams.clip` for `sentence-listen`, `shadow`, `pattern-cloze` examples). Text lookup (`byText`) is a fallback for **sentences** only — never for bare words, since a kana key would collapse homophones.

**AudioService extension** (`src/shared/audio.ts`, contract): `SpeakOptions.clip?`, `speaker?` (the line's speaker label or the text's `narrator`, resolved by the pack to the cast voice; the synth path keeps `voice: 'a' | 'b'` from `voiceSlots()`), `onProgress?`, `clause?: [number, number]`; `AudioService.source?: Signal<'clips' | 'synth' | 'none'>`, `clipFor?(key)`, `packReady?`, `clips?: ClipPlayer`. All of them are optional like `canSpeak`, so `{ ...nullAudio, hasJaVoice: signal(true) }` doubles keep working. The idle `source` (before the first `speak()`) is a derived computed that follows state — the pack turning `ready`, `settings.audioSource` pack ↔ device, voices arriving — so `audioSourceOf(services.audio)` is capability-correct at every render; `clipFor(key)` applies the same gate as `speak()` (`undefined` unless `clips.ready === 'ready'` and `settings.audioSource !== 'device'`; `packChosenIn(settings)` is the one body behind `canSpeak`, the idle source, the clip branch and `clipFor`). `servicesVersion` (a `Signal<number>` bumped by every `installServices()` / `resetServices()`) is what a page reads in render to re-render when the real audio service lands after first paint — `src/app/hooks.ts` `useAudioService()` / `useClipPlayer()`; `main.tsx installAudio()` bumps it on every outcome, calling `installServices({})` when the audio barrel fails to load or `createWebAudio` throws, so a failed boot settles the pack card instead of leaving it "looking" for the life of the page. In `tts.ts` **`speak()` resolves the clip before the synth guard**: `cancelAll` → resolve the cue (clip key / speaker / `byText` for a sentence) → if `settings.audioSource === 'pack'`, `clips.ready === 'ready'` and the cue hits, play it and return → else the existing `if (disposed || !synth || !Utterance) return` guard and the utterance path. Today that guard is the first statement (`tts.ts:506`), which would silence a kiosk browser that has a pack and no `speechSynthesis`. One `cancelAll` owns both queues. `canSpeak = (hasJaVoice || (packReady && settings.audioSource !== 'device')) && audible` — a learner who chose the device voice is never told a silent pack can speak — and the former direct `hasJaVoice` readers (Onboarding's `voiceInstallNeeded`, Library's `voiceMissing`, Settings' `packSpeaks`, Shadow's muted note and `installAdvice(a, ua, s?)` in `src/audio/strings.ts`) read the contract body **`packWillSpeak(a, packChosen)`** = `packChosen && (packReady || audioSourceOf(a) === 'clips')`, so a device with a pack and no Japanese voice gets audio without the install nag, and a device-voice learner with a ready pack still sees it.

**Caching.** The service worker **does not intercept `audio/sprites/`** (pass-through to the network): a cache-first rule there would grow Cache Storage without bound and outside the learner's control (Radio alone streams up to 45 min of sprites per sitting). `clips.ts` owns the Cache API bucket `tsuzuku-audio-v1` directly: `cache.match(url)` first, network second, and `cache.put` **only** for (a) the current station's idle prefetch set (1–3 MB; skipped while `dataSaverOn()` — `settings.dataSaver || saveDataRequested()`, §13) and (b) belts the learner downloaded from Settings. Pruning is a **set difference against `manifest.files[].path`**, never a version-prefix match, so an unchanged sprite survives every release. `audio/manifest.json` and `audio/<audioVersion>/*.json` go through the SW as stale-while-revalidate. "Download this belt's audio" fetches the belt's sprites into the bucket with a progress bar; Settings reports real usage from `cacheUsage()`; the Safari note says downloaded audio may be purged after 7 days without a visit unless the app is installed to the Home Screen.

**Media Session.** Radio sets `navigator.mediaSession.metadata` (title `titleJa`, artist `speaker · synthetic · VOICEVOX:<character>`, album 茶屋ラジオ · station; no artwork yet — `docs/BUILD_STATUS.md` Known gaps) and play / pause / previoustrack / nexttrack / seekbackward handlers. With the screen off only whole-text tracks play; sentence sets need seeking and stay foreground.

## 9. Karaoke and timing

A story cue carries `m`: cumulative mora boundaries in ms from `(Σ consonant_length + vowel_length) / speedScale + prePhonemeLength`, pause morae folded into the preceding boundary (measured 6.38 s estimated vs 6.34 s actual), and `c`: clause boundaries as mora indexes at `pause_mora` and 。、. Token *i* owns morae `[k_i, k_{i+1})` where `k` is the cumulative `moraCount(toKana(tok.ja))` over `segmentsToTokens(line.ja, line.tokens)`; V13 guarantees the token readings concatenate to `line.kana`, which is the order `m` was aligned to (`src/input/listening/karaoke.ts tokenMoraSpans`; punctuation tokens own zero morae). **One mora body everywhere:** `speechMorae(kana)` / `RE_NOT_MORA` in `src/shared/jp.ts` (small kana folded; `、。！？「」『』〜…・` and whitespace dropped; `~`, the NFKC fold of ～, joins the clause marks) is what the validator's V41 `lineMorae` and V18 `moraCount`, the linker's pool `morae`, the clip indexes' `m` / `c` cues (`moraTotal` / `clauseBoundaries` over one `spokenKana` string, so `c[last] === moraTotal` by construction) and the Arena's clause cut (`speechMoraSplit` / `speechMoraCount` in karaoke.ts, `SentenceListen`) all count — a clause the index names is the clause the drill dictates.

Three highlight levels, two shown at once: **line** (the existing `data-speaking` rule), **token** (a soft sweep that fills each `.ruby__token` over its span, shown at ≥ 1.0×), **mora** (pitch dots above the ruby from the engine's per-mora `pitch`, only in slow mode — per-mora flashing at speed reads as noise). The synth path stays line-level (no token timing exists there) and the transcript says nothing about it. `clauses` make long lines dictable: `sentence-listen` and the Arena dictate one clause of a line > `DICTATION_MAX_MORAE` (22) instead of skipping the line (600 lines exceed 40 morae), loop-a-line becomes loop-a-clause, and a missed question with `lineRef` replays that line.

## 12. The 耳 cap, the ledger, the honest share

**耳 axis** (`store/perapera.ts`, replaces `05 §11`'s row; the constants are `EAR_CAP_STEP_HOURS = [7.5, 15]`, `EAR_CAP_STEPS = [70, 85, 100]`, `EAR_GOAL_HOURS = 15`, `COLD_SETS_MIN = 10`, `COLD_PASS_TARGET = 0.8`):

```
tier      = min(settings.speedTier, max(1, stats.bestCleanTier))       // the preset, capped by the tier the learner has shown
base      = 20 · tier · clamp(dictationAcc / 0.9, 0.5, 1)
comp      = coldSets ≥ 10 ? clamp(coldPassRate / 0.8, 0.5, 1) : 1        // material understood on first hearing, either surface
authentic = externalListenWatchHours + stats.listeningAuthenticMinutes / 60
cap       = authentic < 7.5 ? 70 : authentic < 15 ? 85 : 100            // EAR_CAP_STEP_HOURS — heard in the dojo or outside, both count
ear       = min(cap, base · comp)
```

`bestCleanTier` (new `STAT_KEY`) is the highest tier at which the learner has passed a `listening:set` with every question right (`total ≥ 3`, `correct === total`; tier = nearest `SPEED_TIER_RATES` entry to the set's `rate`); existing saves are seeded with their current `speedTier` at migration so nobody's radar drops on upgrade, new saves start at 1. `coldSets` / `coldSetsPassed` count **first hearings on either surface**: an Arena set whose text was not in `progress.listened` (pass → both), and a Radio cold track whose gist question was answered (correct → both, wrong → `coldSets` only); a Radio cold track with the question skipped counts nothing. Without this the factor would measure Radio usage, not comprehension — a Radio listener hears every text before its Arena set.

`listeningAuthenticMinutes` counts (a) `listening:set` minutes (the set's wall-clock `minutes`, as the ledger already counts them) on **authentic-style texts** — `isAuthenticText`: `bridge`, an `AUTHENTIC_GENRES` piece, or one of the 29 existing casual non-bridge dialogues from Station 10 — passed (≥ `PASS_RATIO`) at rate ≥ 1.0, and (b) `listen:played` seconds on such texts at ≥ 1.0× whose gist question was answered correctly, on **cold hearings and re-hearings alike** (Radio asks it on both, §10.2). The inventory, computed from the corpus (`docs/listening-report.md` §3, content `c-22578c2f0f`): bridge texts 25 ≈ 1.0 h + casual non-bridge dialogues 29 ≈ 0.6 h + authentic pieces (`audioFirst` with `isAuthenticText`: the Orange `casual-talk` and the Blue–Black `interview` / `news` / `drama` / `debate` / `lecture` / `casual-talk` pieces) 48 ≈ 2.4 h ≈ **4.0 h distinct** (the walk measures 4.02 h; `DISTINCT_AUTHENTIC_HOURS` stays pinned at 4.0, inside the test's 0.15 tolerance). An Arena set runs ≈ 2.5× the speech length (`ARENA_SET_FACTOR`), so sets alone give ≈ 10.0 h; two Radio re-hearings at ≥ 1.0× with the gist question (`RADIO_REHEARINGS`) add ≈ 8.0 h — **`REACHABLE_IN_APP_HOURS` = round(4.0 × (2.5 + 2)) = 18**. So **85 is reachable from Arena sets alone** (≈ 10 h ≥ 7.5), **100 needs the re-hearings** (≈ 18 h ≥ 15) or outside hours, and a learner who skips the re-hearings has 85 as the in-app ceiling. `src/store/perapera.test.ts` walks the corpus inventory and asserts the walked figure never exceeds `REACHABLE_IN_APP_HOURS` and equals it once every quota piece exists, so the sentence cannot drift from the content. The Records-page explain line is built from these strings in `src/store/defs-strings.ts`, quoted verbatim — the slots are `{tier}` / `{acc}`; then `{tier, rate}` when the dial sits above the shown tier; `{passed, sets, rate}` once a first hearing exists; and `{cap, goal, app, ext, reach}` = the current cap, `EAR_GOAL_HOURS` 15, the dojo's authentic hours to a tenth, the ledger's outside listen + watch hours to a tenth and `REACHABLE_IN_APP_HOURS` 18, shown while `authentic < 15`:

> `explainEar` — "Listening tier {tier} of 5 at {acc} % dictation accuracy. Synthetic: every voice in the dojo is a VOICEVOX character voice. The ledger's outside hours are real people."
> `explainEarNoData` — "Listening tier {tier} of 5. No dictation lines yet, so the floor applies."
> `explainEarTier` — "Tier {tier} of 5: the fastest clean set so far ran at {rate}×. The dial may sit higher; the radar reads the tier you have shown."
> `explainEarComp` — "Understood on first hearing: {passed} of {sets} texts ({rate} %). Counts from ten first hearings; 80 % reads as full, 0 % as half."

For a new learner the last line therefore reads "Held at 70 until 15 h of authentic-style listening: 0 h in the dojo (…) + 0 h outside so far. The dojo alone can carry you to about 18 h; the rest is the world." (`tenth()` in `src/store/perapera.ts` rounds to a tenth and `fill()` prints the number as JavaScript does — `0 h`, `14.9 h`, never `0.0 h`; `perapera.test.ts` asserts "0 h in the dojo".) The flag stays `S.flagSynthetic` "(synthetic)" (`src/store/defs-strings.ts`); the Records page explains it with `S.peraperaFlagSynthetic` (`src/records/strings.ts`), the same sentence `S.explainEar` carries: "Synthetic: every voice in the dojo is a VOICEVOX character voice. **Ledger.** `listening:set` (Arena, Ear Walk) logs a `listen` row with `source: app` at whole minutes, one row per set, as it always did; `listen:played { source: 'radio' | 'readalong', seconds }` logs `playedMinutes(seconds)` — `seconds / 60` to a tenth of a minute (`MINUTE_DECIMALS` 1; under 3 s is dropped) — and **coalesces** (`withCoalescedEntry`, `store/ledger.ts`): when the row a new row would follow (the last row dated today or earlier; a row dated after today — a restored backup, a moved clock — is skipped, never merged into) is today's `listen` / `app` row without a note, the track's tenths are added into it, otherwise a row is appended. A track after an Arena set merges into the set's row: the ledger records heard minutes per day, not per surface. `ledger:entry` still carries each track's own minutes (0 XP: `xp.ts` ignores `source: app`); no listening event writes a note (bookmarks are the learner's own words). Because today's row grows, `backup.mergeLedger` no longer unions app rows by identity (an earlier export of the same device would be counted twice): app rows (source app, no note) are compared **per day and kind, and the side with the larger sum keeps its rows** (tie → this browser's; the never-inflating rule `mergeDaily` and the monthly totals already use), while outside and noted rows still union by identity. Drill audio (Audio Flash, dictation, Shadow) is form work and is not ledger input. `weekProgress` and the weekly external target keep their meaning (`05 §9`: outside hours); `appListeningHours(m)` / `weekAppMinutes(m, today)` feed the informational line "In the dojo this week: {h} h heard" on the Ledger and Station pages, and the Ledger page renders app listen rows to a tenth.

**Honest share** (the Ledger page and the About page — `src/records/strings.ts` and `src/app/pages/strings.ts`, identical; it replaced "the app's audio is a text-to-speech voice" in `01 §2`; quoted verbatim): "Every voice in the dojo is synthetic: VOICEVOX character voices, pre-rendered, clear and consistent on purpose. That covers about two-thirds of the weekly listening target at White and Yellow and all of it at Orange, about a quarter at Green, a sixth at Blue and Purple and a tenth at Brown and Black. Real people mumble, overlap and joke; the ledger’s outside hours exist for that, and the 1,500-hour goal still needs the world." (`WEEKLY_TARGET_HOURS`, `src/store/ledger.ts`: White 0.5 · Yellow–Orange 1 · Green 3 · Blue–Purple 5 · Brown 7 · Black 8 h a week; `05 §9`.) The per-belt sentence is derived from connected speech only — word clips are drills, not listening. ## 15. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| VOICEVOX misreads kanji (7.5 % of sampled lines) | per-clip mora verification + four-step ladder; `skipped.jsonl` reviewed per render; the device voice covers a skipped clip (the three-tier dry run skips 2 of 49,439 clips; the real engine's acceptance of the new んっ / ーっ partner strings and the 13 first-pass `verifyMorae` failures is unverified until the Phase 3 render) |

| 15 h of authentic listening (the 100 step) unreachable from sets alone | the cap steps at 7.5 h and 15 h; sets alone reach ≈ 10 h (85), the re-hearings ≈ 18 h; the explain line states the reachable figure (`REACHABLE_IN_APP_HOURS` 18) and the corpus-walking test keeps it honest |
